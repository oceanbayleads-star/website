About us

Built on 20 years of construction & custom woodworking

Ocean Bay Interiors was founded to fix a major problem in the cabinetry industry: low-quality, mass-produced imports taking over the market.

Today we're a family-owned shop with two decades of hands-on experience. We choose solid wood and heavy-duty hardware for durability, not margins, and you work directly with the craftsmen who design, build, and install your cabinets.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Yes. Ocean Bay Interiors is fully licensed and insured for all residential custom carpentry, fabrication, and structural installation work in South Carolina.

Timeline depends on project scope, but most residential projects take between 3 to 6 weeks from final 3D design approval and material selection to completed installation. Because we manufacture everything in-house, we control production schedules without waiting on overseas shipments.

Stock cabinets come in rigid 3-inch increments, leaving wasted filler space, thin backing panels, and low-grade hardware. Custom cabinets are built to the exact millimeter of your room layout, utilizing 100% of available space with solid hardwood components and reinforced joints that last for decades.

We stand behind our work long after installation. If a hinge requires adjusting, a drawer slide needs tuning, or you have questions months down the road, simply call us. We provide dedicated ongoing support to ensure your cabinetry functions like new.
