Privacy policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Ocean Bay Interiors ("we", "us") operates oceanbayinteriors.com. This policy explains what information we collect when you use the site, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you submit the consultation form we collect your name, phone number, email address and the project details you type in. We use this only to respond to your request and schedule your consultation.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites we may collect usage data through cookies and similar technologies, such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser type, and referring site. We use Google Tag Manager and may use Google Analytics and Google Ads conversion tracking for this purpose.

How we use information

  • To reply to your consultation request by phone or email.
  • To prepare estimates, designs and schedule installation.
  • To understand how the site is used and improve it.
  • To measure the results of our advertising.

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run the site or communicate with you (for example, our form and email providers), and when required by law.

Cookies and analytics

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Google's use of data from analytics and advertising cookies is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Text messages and calls

By providing your phone number you agree that we may call or text you about your request. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text to opt out.

Data retention and security

We keep consultation requests for as long as needed to serve you and meet our legal and accounting obligations. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Your choices

You can ask us to update or delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by calling (908) 251-2855 or emailing contact@oceanbayinteriors.com.

Children

The site is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.
